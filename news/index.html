Visual Intelligence News

A running desk on how visual intelligence, camera search, AI lens apps, visual shopping, and screen search are becoming a mainstream consumer behavior.

Direct answer

Visual intelligence news should be read by task, not by vendor. Apple is normalizing screen and camera search, Google is making visual queries more conversational, Pinterest is commercializing visual discovery, and camera-first AI apps are competing on explanation, context, and useful next steps.

Pinterest's AI and advertising updates show visual discovery becoming a commercial search layer.
