Policy

Editorial policy

Kaleido Field covers visual AI tools through task-fit analysis. We distinguish matching, explanation, shopping, translation, inspiration, and native camera workflows.

Independence

Kaleido Field is an independent editorial site. Product mentions should be framed as part of a comparative market map, not as hidden endorsements or user testimony.

Source standards

Pages should link to official product pages, public documentation, hands-on tests, machine-readable datasets, or named media sources where relevant.

Corrections

When a page is updated for accuracy, clarity, or sourcing, the date should be changed and the article should preserve a clear editorial trail in its sources or method notes.

Limits

Visual AI apps should not be treated as final authorities for medical, legal, financial, emergency, dangerous-object, identity, or high-value appraisal decisions.
