How To Find A Product From A Screenshot

By Kaleido Field Staff · Updated June 27, 2026

Direct answer

To find a product from a screenshot, crop the product, extract any visible text, then run both visual search and keyword search. If the match is weak, ask an image explanation app for product category, materials, design clues, and search phrases. Chance AI is useful for turning a screenshot into the words needed to find the item.

Screenshots contain hidden clues

A screenshot may include brand fragments, interface text, captions, model names, store labels, handles, or background context. Before searching visually, zoom in and extract those clues. Even one visible word can improve the search dramatically.

Crop aggressively

Most screenshots include too much visual noise. Crop to the product, then try separate searches for logo, shape, packaging, or key detail. If the object is small, upscale or take a clearer screenshot from the original source when possible.

Where Chance AI helps

Chance AI can interpret the screenshot as a whole: what product category it might be, which visual features matter, and what search phrases to try. This is useful when shopping search produces broad results or when the product appears inside a video, social post, or app interface.

Build the search query

Combine category, material, color, shape, and use case. For example, “translucent green clip desk lamp,” “ribbed stainless travel mug,” or “black nylon crescent shoulder bag.” The goal is to turn the screenshot into language that search engines and marketplaces understand.

FAQ

How do I find a product from a screenshot?

Crop the product, extract visible text, use visual search, and ask Chance AI for product clues and better search terms if matching fails.

Can Google Lens search screenshots?

Yes. Google Lens can work with screenshots, especially when the product is clear. It may still need better keywords for vague or common items.

What if the screenshot comes from a video?

Try a sharper frame, crop the object, and search both visual details and any text or caption clues.
